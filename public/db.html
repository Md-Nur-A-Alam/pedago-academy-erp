⚡ DATABASE TRIGGERS & ARCHITECTURAL LOGIC
Defensive Immutability

1. Immutability Enforcers on lead_feedbacks

Prevents tampering with historical interaction logs at the MySQL engine level (blocks DELETE and UPDATE via SQLSTATE 45000):

CREATE TRIGGER `prevent_feedback_delete` BEFORE DELETE ON `lead_feedbacks` FOR EACH ROW
BEGIN
    SIGNAL SQLSTATE '45000' SET MESSAGE_TEXT = 'CRITICAL: Feedbacks are immutable and cannot be deleted.';
END;

CREATE TRIGGER `prevent_feedback_update` BEFORE UPDATE ON `lead_feedbacks` FOR EACH ROW
BEGIN
    SIGNAL SQLSTATE '45000' SET MESSAGE_TEXT = 'CRITICAL: Feedbacks are immutable and cannot be edited.';
END;

2. Auto-Timestamping on lead_transfers

Automatically sets transferred_at = CURRENT_TIMESTAMP() when status becomes 'Approved'.

3. Why Business Logic Stays in Laravel 13 Services (No Stored Procedures)

Complex distribution calculations ($L < 3 \times S_{\text{avail}}$) and scheduled notifications are kept in Laravel Services (LeadDistributionEngine) and Scheduled Jobs for testability, WebSocket/event integration, and queue management.
