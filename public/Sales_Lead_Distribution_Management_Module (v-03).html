01

User Roles & Operational Hierarchy

Level 1 • Global Control
Admin
Universal platform control, global configuration parameters, and emergency overrides.
  • Controls master Automatic Engine Toggle (ON / OFF).
  • Configures global batch size $n$ (default 3) and shift cut-off thresholds.
  • Assigns teams to Supervisors and Team Leaders.
  • Resolves duplicate conflicts from the Admin Duplicate Review Queue.
Level 2 • Multi-Team Oversight
Supervisor (1:N Teams)
Assigned by Admin to oversee multiple designated sales teams simultaneously.
  • Supervises assigned 1:N teams with full cross-team visibility.
  • Monitors executive batches, overdue follow-ups, and transfer requests.
  • Resolves edge cases in the duplicate review module for assigned teams.
Level 3 • Single Team
Team Leader
Direct manager of a specific sales squad of Sales Executives.
  • Monitors team live batch completion, overdue follow-ups, and `Lazy` alerts.
  • Dual-approval partner for cross-team lead transfer requests.
  • Performs operational manual lead reassignments for absent members.
Level 4 • Execution
Sales Executive
Field sales agent dedicated to lead communication and conversions.
  • Works strictly on assigned $n$-Lead active batch and due follow-up tasks.
  • Mandatory feedback post-call; completes batch to pull next batch.
  • Generates Student Enrollment & Installment plan links upon conversion.
  • In-context access to previous course history for assigned contacts only.
Specialized • Finance & Reconciliation
Accounts / Finance Department
Dedicated finance desk handling payment confirmation, slip verification, and student admission sign-off.
  • Monitors the Payment Under Review queue for both Online Gateway and Manual deposit submissions.
  • Cross-references bank statements / MFS statements with submitted TrxID and screenshot slips.
  • Approving payment officially triggers Lead Batch Completion for the sales executive and issues official Money Receipt.

03

Dynamic Shift Roster & Availability State Machine

Each executive has individualized shift parameters (shift_start_time, shift_end_time, is_on_duty_today). The system executes continuous state transitions:

Status State Lead Push Eligibility State Trigger & Business Behavior
Available Eligible for Batch On-duty, within shift window before cutoff, and current active batch has 0 pending items.
Busy Ineligible Executive has 1 to $n$ pending items in their current active batch. New batch assignment is blocked.
Off Duty Ineligible Outside shift hours, on scheduled weekly leave, or current time is within 30 minutes of shift end (Cutoff).
Unavailable Ineligible Temporarily paused by Team Leader/Admin for meetings, training, or short official breaks.
Lazy Blocked Penalty Triggered when follow-ups are overdue for $t$ hours with no feedback. Disqualified from new batches until cleared.
Resigned Permanent Ban Former employee. All remaining leads unassigned; never assigned any future batches.
04

Distribution Engine & Batch Allocation Algorithm

The Scarcity Degradation Rules ($L < 3 \times S_{\text{avail}}$)

Let $L$ be the total unassigned leads in the queue, and $S_{\text{avail}}$ be the total currently available sales executives.

  • Normal Supply ($L \ge 3 \times S_{\text{avail}}$): Distribute standard batch $n = 3$ to every available executive.
  • Moderate Scarcity ($2 \times S_{\text{avail}} \le L < 3 \times S_{\text{avail}}$):
    • Step 1: Assign $n = 2$ leads to each available executive.
    • Step 2: Distribute remaining $L - (2 \times S_{\text{avail}})$ leads single man single lead (1-by-1 Round Robin) until exhausted.
  • Extreme Scarcity ($L < 2 \times S_{\text{avail}}$): Distribute 1 lead per person Round Robin until $L = 0$.

End-to-End Conversion, Enrollment & Installment Lifecycle

1
Sales Executive Converts Lead & Generates Form Lead Form Ready

Executive enters Course, Fee, Scholarship %, and Installment schedule breakdown. System generates a tamper-proof cryptographic link: /enroll/verify/{secure_uuid}.

2
Customer Portal Review & Verification Enrolled - Pending Payment

Student opens link on WhatsApp/SMS. Can edit spelling of name/email. Examines course breakdown and installment terms. (Sales batch task remains occupied).

3
Payment Submission (bKash, SSLCommerz, or Manual TrxID) Payment Under Review

Customer pays via bKash/SSLCommerz gateway, or submits Manual MFS/Bank Transaction ID and/or Deposit Screenshot. Form submitted to Finance.

4
Accounts / Finance Verification & Approval Batch Completed & Money Receipt

Accounts audits bank/MFS statement and approves transaction. Executive's batch slot is officially released & completed! Official Money Receipt issued.

5
Installment Tracking & Automated $T-2$ Alerts Converted Follow-up

If paid in installments, 2 days before the next installment due date, the system auto-routes it to the executive's Converted Follow-up queue. Full payment marks lead as Fully Completed.

06

Dual-Approval Cross-Team Transfer Engine

Dual Team Leader Approval

Transfer across squads requires explicit approval from both the Sender's Team Leader and the Receiver's Team Leader.

Zero Operational Freezing

The lead is never locked or frozen during the pending transfer. The current owner continues handling calls and feedback until transfer completes.

⚡ 7-Day Auto-Transfer Fallback Rule

If a transfer request stays in pending state for 7 calendar days without rejection, the ERP automated cron job automatically authorizes and executes the transfer, preventing management bottlenecks.

07

Restricted Lead Context Search & Anti-Poaching

Security Policy Enforcement

To protect sales integrity and eliminate customer lead poaching, executives cannot search arbitrary numbers across the central database.

Direct API access (`/api/v1/leads/{id}`) is guarded by LeadPolicy. Guessing lead IDs returns 403 Forbidden.

"View Past Inquiries & Course History" Modal

Accessible exclusively on the details modal of an actively assigned lead. When clicked:

  • Verifies caller is current active owner.
  • Fetches all past inquiry history for matching primary or secondary phone.
  • Shows past interested courses and notes without revealing unrelated leads.

Complete Database Schema Architecture (`pedago_erp_db`)

-- Database: pedago_erp_db (MySQL utf8mb4)
CREATE DATABASE IF NOT EXISTS `pedago_erp_db` CHARACTER SET utf8mb4;
USE `pedago_erp_db`;

-- 1. System Settings (Engine Toggle & Global Constants)
CREATE TABLE `system_settings` (
    `id` BIGINT UNSIGNED AUTO_INCREMENT PRIMARY KEY,
    `key` VARCHAR(100) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    `value` TEXT NOT NULL,
    `updated_at` TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP ON UPDATE CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- 2. User Shifts (Dynamic Working Window & Availability)
CREATE TABLE `user_shifts` (
    `id` BIGINT UNSIGNED AUTO_INCREMENT PRIMARY KEY,
    `user_id` BIGINT UNSIGNED NOT NULL UNIQUE,
    `shift_start_time` TIME DEFAULT '10:00:00',
    `shift_end_time` TIME DEFAULT '18:00:00',
    `is_on_duty_today` BOOLEAN DEFAULT TRUE,
    `availability_status` ENUM('Available', 'Busy', 'Unavailable', 'Off Duty', 'Lazy', 'Resigned') DEFAULT 'Off Duty',
    `updated_at` TIMESTAMP NULL
);

-- 3. Core Leads Table
CREATE TABLE `leads` (
    `id` BIGINT UNSIGNED AUTO_INCREMENT PRIMARY KEY,
    `lead_code` VARCHAR(50) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    `customer_name` VARCHAR(150) NOT NULL,
    `primary_phone` VARCHAR(25) NOT NULL,
    `alternate_phone` VARCHAR(25) NULL,
    `status` ENUM('New', 'Assigned', 'Contacted', 'Follow-up', 'Enrolled - Pending Payment', 'Payment Under Review', 'Converted', 'Not Interested', 'Lost') DEFAULT 'New',
    `current_owner_id` BIGINT UNSIGNED NULL,
    `team_id` BIGINT UNSIGNED NULL,
    `created_at` TIMESTAMP NULL
);

-- 4. Enrollments & Installments Table
CREATE TABLE `enrollments` (
    `id` BIGINT UNSIGNED AUTO_INCREMENT PRIMARY KEY,
    `lead_id` BIGINT UNSIGNED NOT NULL UNIQUE,
    `secure_uuid` VARCHAR(64) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    `base_course_fee` DECIMAL(10,2) NOT NULL,
    `final_deal_amount` DECIMAL(10,2) NOT NULL,
    `total_installments` INT UNSIGNED DEFAULT 1,
    `status` ENUM('Pending Payment', 'Payment Under Review', 'Enrolled') DEFAULT 'Pending Payment'
);
